Our Purpose

About the Trust & Temple

The Trust

Sri Kashi Vishveshwara Swami Seva Trust was formed to carry out charitable and religious activities in service of the community and the temple.

Our first commitment is to Sri Kashi Vishveshwara Swami Temple, Devamba Agrahara, where we work alongside the temple trust on both everyday temple seva and long-term development works.

The Temple

Sri Kashi Vishveshwara Swami Temple in Devamba Agrahara is the spiritual heart of this seva. Kashi Vishveshwara is a form of Lord Shiva, and the temple's rhythm follows the traditional cycle of Shiva and Vishnu observances through the Hindu lunar calendar.

The trust supports the temple's daily worship and its ongoing upkeep and development, in coordination with the temple trust.
